# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Rabbit to be a welcoming project for everyone who contributes, regardless of experience
level, background or identity. Technical disagreement is normal and healthy; personal attacks are
not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion focused on the work: code, docs, tests and design.
- Help newcomers; everyone started somewhere.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or other conduct that would reasonably be considered
  inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile or by
opening a private security advisory on this repository.
